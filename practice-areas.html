  • Writ Petitions (Article 226/227) against arbitrary state/administrative action.
  • Public Interest Litigation (PIL).
  • High Court Statutory Criminal & Civil Appeals.
  • Civil Revisions and Review Petitions.
  • Quashing of FIRs (Section 482 CrPC).

2. Corporate Retainership & Commercial Law

  • Outsourced In-House Counsel (Daily Legal Operations).
  • Commercial Contract Drafting (NDAs, SLAs, Joint Ventures, EPCs).
  • NCLT & NCLAT Litigation (Company Law Disputes).
  • Insolvency & Bankruptcy Code (IBC) Proceedings.
  • Statutory Corporate Audits & Labor Law Compliance (EPF, ESI).

3. Banking, Finance & Debt Recovery

  • DRT (Debt Recovery Tribunal) & DRAT Litigation.
  • SARFAESI Act Enforcement and Defense against Bank Auctions.
  • Cheque Bounce Prosecution & Defense (Sec 138 NI Act).
  • Legal Demand Notices and Rapid Dues Recovery.
  • Asset Tracing and Financial Fraud Litigation.

4. Real Estate, Revenue & Property Law

  • Land Mutation (Dakhil Kharij): End-to-end processing and dispute resolution.
  • Property Registration: Sale Deeds, Lease Agreements, Gift Deeds.
  • Title Suit Litigation, Title Chain Verification, and Jamabandi checks.
  • RERA Disputes (Builder-Buyer, delayed possession).
  • Land Encroachment, Partition Suits, and Boundary Disputes.

5. Municipal, Local Body & Panchayat Law

  • Municipal Corporation Disputes (Patna Municipal Corporation, etc.).
  • Building Plan Approvals and Sanction Disputes.
  • Defense against Municipal Demolition/Encroachment Notices.
  • Property Tax and Municipal Taxation Disputes.
  • Trade License procurement and litigation.
  • Panchayati Raj Act matters and local election disputes.

6. Drafting, Notary & Legal Administration

  • Affidavits: Drafting and execution of all types of legal affidavits.
  • Notary Public Services: Document attestation and notarization.
  • Power of Attorney: Drafting and registering GPA and SPA.
  • Wills & Trusts: Drafting, registration, and Probate of Wills.
  • Procurement of Certified Copies of Court Orders and Judgments.

  • Anticipatory Bail, Regular Bail, and Transit Bail.

  • Severe Crimes (Murder, Attempt to Murder, Kidnapping).
  • Customs, FEMA, DRI Seizures, and GST Evasion.

8. Civil Litigation & Dispute Resolution

  • Suits for Specific Performance of Contracts.
  • Suits for Permanent and Mandatory Injunctions.
  • Money Recovery Suits.
  • Arbitration and Conciliation Proceedings.

  • Mutual Consent and Contested Divorce.
  • Defense against 498A (Cruelty) & Dowry Prohibition Act.
